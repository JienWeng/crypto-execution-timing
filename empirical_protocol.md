# Locked empirical protocol — execution-weighted calibration

Recorded before computing execution-policy outcomes in August 2026. The earlier June/July scalar return diagnostic is known and disclosed; this is not a preregistered study. Author authorized autonomous completion of the research and manuscript.

Primary: BTCUSDT spot, June 2026 training, July calibration, August evaluation. Same downloaded and hashed one-minute Binance klines. UTC daily episodes start at minute 5,35,...,1415. Signal uses five completed bars immediately before decision. Trades occur at the opens of minutes i+1,...,i+15, providing a one-minute information-to-execution delay. These 15 equal slots finish before the next episode. Open prices proxy execution; no quote/depth/impact data.

Signal: signed base-volume imbalance. Fit an intercept and slope for each of 15 future open-price returns relative to the decision open, in bps, using June only. Nominal signal forecast is slope times (current imbalance minus June mean imbalance); omit the unconditional intercept to isolate signal conditioning. No horizon, predictor or market search. Fit a sell/liquidation schedule using these expected price levels and a convex quadratic objective.

Normalize parent quantity to 1. For u on the simplex, q=1-Lu, realized objective is -p'u + eta*N*u'u + r/N*q'q, where p is the vector of open-price returns in bps. This differs from monetary shortfall by the explicitly stated inventory penalty; the price term is negative for sales. Impact cost at TWAP is eta bps. Constant fees/spreads cancel in paired equal-volume comparisons and are not a source of the reported gain. Primary eta=2 bps, r=1 bps. Scenarios eta in {0.5,2,5}, r in {0,1}; no selection among scenarios after evaluation.

Baseline u0 minimizes impact plus inventory penalty on the simplex. Nominal endpoint u1 minimizes that objective minus forecast sale proceeds. Solve both constrained quadratic programs numerically and check KKT residuals. δ=u1-u0. u(w)=u0+wδ, 0<=w<=1, is feasible by convexity. All policies complete exactly; neither endpoint is produced by pointwise clipping.

Episode improvement decomposes exactly as w*R_i-w^2*C_i, C_i=eta*N*δ'δ+r/N*(Lδ)'(Lδ), R_i=δ'p_i-2*eta*N*u0'δ+2*r/N*q0'(Lδ). For nonzero mean C, calibration-month optimal fixed weight is clip(mean R/(2 mean C),0,1). Use 2,000 whole-UTC-day bootstrap resamples of July to obtain the 5th percentile of the untruncated ratio. Proposed conservative weight is that percentile clipped to [0,1]. These percentile bounds do not establish future-regime coverage.

Comparators: baseline (weight 0), full signal (1), fixed half (0.5), July plug-in weight, July conservative weight, and TWAP. No claim that the proposed weight dominates the plug-in or half weight. Weight fixed throughout August; no future data or within-August updating. Report all weights and their untruncated bootstrap intervals.

Primary effect: August paired average risk-adjusted objective gain in bps against baseline. Also report paired monetary-shortfall gain, 5th percentile of episode objective improvement, and paired differences of conservative against half and plug-in. For uncertainty use 2,000 day bootstrap resamples of August with 2.5/97.5 percentiles and fixed seed 20260919. Resample full day totals/counts to preserve episode weighting. Six cost scenarios are descriptive sensitivity analysis, not six independent confirmatory discoveries. Positive gain means lower cost. Training seed and calibration bootstrap seed 20260918. No performance-based changes after the August run. Code bugs may be fixed with an explicit change record.

No empirical OU assumption is imposed: the 15-slot forecast profile is learned by separate linear regressions. The OU model is a theoretical bridge to the original preprint, not claimed to be a fitted description of BTC. Endogenous price impact, fills, changing spreads, fees proportional to different realized notionals and participation caps are outside the empirical identification scope.
