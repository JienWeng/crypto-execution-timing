# Closest prior work and claim boundary

Checked 20 September 2026 using primary author-institution metadata/abstract.

Kweon, Sunmin, Yonghwan Yim and Seungki Min (2024). Optimizing Sequential Predictions for Order Execution: a Decision Focused Learning Approach. ICAIF 2024, pp. 719–727. DOI https://doi.org/10.1145/3677052.3698665. Primary record: https://snu.elsevierpure.com/en/publications/optimizing-sequential-predictions-for-order-execution-a-decision-/

This paper already applies decision-focused learning to time-series forecasts used for adaptive liquidation under stochastic liquidity, with US-stock experiments. Its existence materially narrows the novelty argument. We cannot claim the first integration of decision-focused forecasts and execution, or that forecast-error/decision-loss mismatch was overlooked. Full text was not reviewed here; metadata and abstract support this limited comparison.

Our planned distinction is frozen candidate selection on a calibration month, with a shared no-signal alternative, endpoint/path/within-path-contrast/objective comparison, and untouched-in-this-project historical asset transfer. No claim of comparable method implementation or superiority to Kweon et al. is justified without reproducing their algorithm. The prospective economic question remains whether this simpler selector improves decisions in the stated model; data cannot establish actual fills or market-impact costs.
